

## Comparison Table

|Aspect|Intranet|Internet|
|---|---|---|
|**Definition**|A private network restricted to an organization|A global public network connecting millions of networks worldwide|
|**Access**|Restricted to authorized users (employees, members)|Open to anyone with a connection|
|**Ownership**|Owned and managed by a single organization|Not owned by any single entity; decentralized|
|**Scope**|Limited to a specific organization or institution|Worldwide, no geographical limit|
|**Security**|High — protected by firewalls, login credentials|Comparatively lower — more exposed to threats|
|**Content**|Internal data: policies, memos, internal tools|Public data: websites, services, social media, etc.|
|**Users**|Limited number (employees/members only)|Unlimited number of users globally|
|**Purpose**|Internal communication and resource sharing within an organization|Global communication, information sharing, and services|
|**Example**|A company's internal HR portal or employee wiki|Websites like Google, YouTube, Wikipedia|

---

## Intranet

### Definition

A private, internal network that uses internet-based technologies (TCP/IP, HTTP) but is restricted to members of a specific organization.

### Key Points

- Used for internal communication, file sharing, and collaboration.
- Protected behind firewalls and requires authentication (login credentials).
- Hosts internal resources like employee directories, HR portals, internal wikis, and company announcements.
- Not accessible to the general public.

---

## Internet

### Definition

A vast, publicly accessible global network that connects millions of smaller networks, devices, and servers around the world.

### Key Points

- No single owner; it is decentralized and maintained by many organizations and service providers.
- Accessible to anyone with an internet connection.
- Hosts websites, email services, social media, and countless online applications.
- Comparatively more exposed to security threats due to its open nature.

---

## Related Term: Extranet

An **extranet** is a controlled private network that allows limited access to _specific_ external users, such as vendors, suppliers, or business partners. It sits conceptually between an intranet and the internet — more open than an intranet, but more restricted than the internet.

---

## Summary

- **Intranet** = Private + Internal + Restricted access
- **Internet** = Public + Global + Open access
- **Extranet** = Semi-private, extends intranet access to trusted outsiders
